# PROLOGUE: BEFORE THE END

## Chapter 1 â€” An Ordinary Life

The year is 2049.

Humanity has made significant technological progress. Space exploration is no longer limited to government agencies. Commercial space travel exists, artificial intelligence is integrated into everyday life, and several permanent research stations operate beyond Earth's atmosphere.

But despite all this progress, humanity still considers itself alone in the universe.

At a university in a Nigerian city, Harper Kollins is an engineering student with an unusual obsession.

He spends more time experimenting with machines, building strange devices and imagining impossible technologies than attending some of his lectures.

Harper is intelligent, stubborn and ambitious. He wants to build something that will outlive him.

His friends think he spends too much time dreaming.

His girlfriend, Nadia, thinks he needs to learn how to live in the present.

**NADIA:** "Do you ever get tired of trying to build the future?"

**HARPER:** "No."

**NADIA:** "You haven't even figured out what you're doing tomorrow."

**HARPER:** "That's because tomorrow hasn't happened yet."

**NADIA:** "You're impossible."

**HARPER:** "And yet, you're still here."

Their relationship is imperfect but genuine. Nadia challenges Harper. She does not simply agree with everything he says. She has ambitions of her own and refuses to become a character whose entire existence revolves around him.

At home, Harper has a different world.

His mother, Dr. Amara Kollins, is a brilliant researcher. She rarely discusses her earlier career, and Harper has never understood why she becomes uncomfortable whenever he asks about her past.

His younger sister, Mira, is fascinated by technology. She constantly experiments with devices she barely understands.

Harper and Mira argue like siblings.

**MIRA:** "You think you're the only person in this house who understands technology?"

**HARPER:** "No. I think I'm the only one who knows what she's doing."

**MIRA:** "Your last invention almost burnt the kitchen."

**HARPER:** "That was a controlled experiment."

**MIRA:** "The kitchen disagrees."

These ordinary moments will become some of Harper's most important memories.

Because this is the last ordinary day of his life.

## Chapter 2 â€” The Signal

Several days before the invasion, strange astronomical events begin appearing across the world.

Satellites lose communication. Space observatories detect unusual gravitational disturbances. Several astronauts report seeing objects moving against the direction of known orbital mechanics.

A research station monitoring deep space receives a repeating signal.

The signal contains seven distinct patterns.

Scientists initially assume it is a natural phenomenon.

But Dr. Amara Kollins recognizes it.

She has heard it before.

Thirty years earlier.

In another part of the galaxy.

Amara secretly retrieves an old device from a locked compartment. The device is an ancient piece of technology built by a civilization humanity has never encountered.

Its display activates.

A message appears:

**GENESIS SIGNAL DETECTED.**

Then another:

**THE SEVEN VECTORS ARE AWAKENING.**

Amara immediately contacts someone.

The communication is brief.

**AMARA:** "You said you would never come here."

A distorted male voice answers.

**VOICE:** "I didn't come for you, Amara."

**AMARA:** "Then why are you sending an invasion fleet?"

Silence.

**VOICE:** "Because something has found us."

The communication ends.

Amara looks toward her children's photographs.

For the first time in decades, she is afraid.

# ACT I â€” THE FALL OF EARTH

## Chapter 3 â€” The First Arrival

It begins without warning.

At 14:32 UTC, every major satellite network experiences simultaneous interference.

Air traffic systems malfunction. Military radar detects objects entering Earth's atmosphere at impossible speeds.

Then the sky changes.

Thousands of enormous structures emerge from spatial distortions above Earth's major cities.

They are not conventional spacecraft.

They resemble enormous metallic organisms, with moving sections, glowing cores and strange geometric patterns.

Their arrival causes electromagnetic disturbances across entire continents.

People stop in the streets.

Some record videos.

Others run.

The first explosion occurs above the Atlantic Ocean.

A massive alien vessel descends through the atmosphere and releases smaller craft.

The invasion begins.

The attacking force belongs to the **Iron Synod**, a militarized interstellar empire.

Its soldiers are known as the Synod Guard.

Their technology is centuries beyond humanity's.

Human military forces attempt to respond, but conventional weapons barely damage the invasion fleet.

In the middle of the chaos, Harper tries to reach his family.

His phone barely works.

He finally receives a call from Mira.

**MIRA:** "Harper! Where are you?"

**HARPER:** "I'm coming home. Where's Mum?"

**MIRA:** "She's downstairs. Something is happening. The televisionâ€”"

An explosion interrupts her.

The call disconnects.

Harper runs.

## Chapter 4 â€” The Abduction

When Harper reaches home, the city is already collapsing.

Buildings are burning. Military aircraft fall from the sky. People flee through streets filled with debris.

He finds Mira and Amara inside their house.

Amara is carrying the ancient device.

**HARPER:** "What the hell is happening?"

**AMARA:** "We need to leave. Now."

**HARPER:** "What is that thing?"

**AMARA:** "Something I should have destroyed a long time ago."

Before Harper can ask another question, the entire building shakes.

A Synod transport descends outside.

Its doors open.

Soldiers enter the house.

Harper attacks the first soldier with a metal object. He manages to damage its helmet, but another soldier throws him against a wall.

Mira tries to activate a nearby machine.

A strange blue energy field erupts.

The soldiers react immediately.

One of them speaks through a translator.

**SYNOD COMMANDER:** "The second compatibility signature has been located."

Amara freezes.

**AMARA:** "No. Not her."

The commander orders his soldiers to take Amara and Mira alive.

Harper struggles to reach them.

**HARPER:** "MUM!"

Amara looks directly at him.

**AMARA:** "Harper! Listen to me! Whatever happens, do not let them activate Genesis!"

The soldiers drag her away.

Mira screams his name.

Harper tries to follow, but a powerful energy blast throws him into the street.

The transport rises.

His mother and sister disappear into the sky.

Harper lies among the rubble.

The invasion continues above him.

## Chapter 5 â€” The Last Recording

Hours pass.

Harper regains consciousness inside a damaged building.

The city is unrecognizable.

He searches for survivors and eventually finds a small emergency communication device.

It contains a recording from Nadia.

**NADIA'S RECORDING:**

"Harper, I don't know if you're alive. I don't know where you are.

Everything is falling apart. There are ships everywhere.

I saw people being taken. They're not killing everyone. They're looking for something.

I think your mother knew this was coming.

I don't know what that means.

If you're alive, please find me.

And Harper...

Don't trust anyone who tells you they know what's happening."

The recording ends.

Harper tries to contact her.

No response.

A military rescue team eventually discovers him.

The team belongs to a classified emergency organization known as the **Vanguard Initiative**.

Harper is evacuated to a mobile military installation called Bastion Omega.

Earth has fallen.

Humanity's survival is now uncertain.

# ACT II â€” THE VANGUARD INITIATIVE

## Chapter 6 â€” Bastion Omega

Bastion Omega is a massive mobile military installation operating beyond Earth's immediate atmosphere.

It houses surviving military personnel, scientists, engineers and civilians.

Harper wakes up in a medical facility.

A man introduces himself as Commander Elias Vance.

**VANCE:** "You're lucky to be alive."

**HARPER:** "Where's my family?"

Vance does not answer immediately.

**HARPER:** "I asked you a question."

**VANCE:** "We don't know."

Harper becomes furious.

He demands access to every available record concerning the abductions.

Vance explains that the invasion force has been selectively capturing individuals.

Scientists. Children. Engineers. People with unusual biological markers.

Harper recognizes the phrase the alien commander used.

Compatibility signature.

He tells Vance about his mother's final warning.

Vance becomes interested.

He takes Harper to a restricted laboratory.

There, scientists examine the device recovered from the ruins of his home.

The device activates when Harper approaches.

A holographic interface appears.

**GENESIS COMPATIBILITY: CONFIRMED.**

The room falls silent.

Vance looks at Harper.

**VANCE:** "Your mother didn't just know about them."

**HARPER:** "What are you talking about?"

**VANCE:** "She was connected to them."

## Chapter 7 â€” The Truth About Earth

Harper learns that Earth was not attacked randomly.

The Synod had been searching for something buried beneath the planet.

An ancient machine called the **Genesis Engine**.

According to surviving research, Earth contains an unusually strong natural connection to a mysterious energy field known as the Vector.

The Vector connects matter, energy, space and consciousness.

The ancient civilization that first discovered it was called the Architects.

They developed technology capable of interacting with the field.

Their greatest invention was the Genesis Engine.

The Engine could connect biological consciousness to the Vector itself.

The Synod wants it.

But the machine requires a compatible living consciousness to activate.

Harper is one such individual.

His sister Mira is another.

Vance offers Harper a position in the Vanguard Initiative.

Harper refuses.

**HARPER:** "I'm not joining your army."

**VANCE:** "Then what are you going to do?"

**HARPER:** "Find my family."

**VANCE:** "You won't survive long enough to find them."

Harper looks at the display showing Earth.

**HARPER:** "Then teach me how."

The Odyssey receives its first assignment.

Harper joins the mission.

The enemy is not simply conquering territory.

A captured Synod soldier reveals something disturbing.

**SYNOD SOLDIER:** "Your world was not selected for destruction."

**HARPER:** "Then why are you taking everyone?"

**SYNOD SOLDIER:** "Because your world contains the key."

After the mission, Harper receives an encrypted transmission.

It is from Nadia.

**NADIA:** "Harper, if you're hearing this, I'm alive.

I found something.

Your mother is not the person you think she is.

And the people who rescued you are hiding something.

# ACT V â€” THE FOUR GREAT SECTORS

## Chapter 12 â€” Terra Novus

The Odyssey arrives at Terra Novus, a devastated world used by the Synod as a military processing centre.

The planet contains abandoned cities, orbital factories, detention facilities and massive alien structures.

Harper discovers that humans are being transported through the facilities.

He finds evidence of experiments involving human biological compatibility.

The team infiltrates a Synod installation.

They encounter Commander Varek, a military officer responsible for managing the human collection programme.

Varek recognizes Harper.

**VAREK:** "The son of Amara Kollins."

**HARPER:** "Where is my mother?"

**VAREK:** "You are remarkably similar to her."

**HARPER:** "Where is she?"

**VAREK:** "You still believe this is about rescuing your family."

Varek reveals that Amara's knowledge is essential to the Genesis Engine.

He refuses to disclose her location.

A major battle begins.

Harper destroys Varek's command vessel, the Reaver.

But before dying, Varek sends a message to the Iron Archon.

**VAREK:** "The son has arrived."

The Odyssey escapes.

Harper now knows the enemy has been expecting him.

## Chapter 13 â€” Solar Corona

The team travels to a dangerous solar system where a gigantic star produces violent energy storms.

Hidden within the system is an ancient Architect observatory.

The observatory contains records of the First Silence.

Harper learns that the Architects discovered something beyond the known universe.

An entity called **The Hollow**.

The Hollow was not a conventional creature.

It was a force capable of consuming collective consciousness.

It could infect networks of connected minds, absorb memories and gradually erase the identities of entire civilizations.

The Architects attempted to stop it.

They failed.

The First Silence occurred when entire civilizations disappeared without leaving physical evidence.

The remaining Architects divided their technology into seven relics.

The Seven Vectors.

These relics were designed to prevent the Hollow from accessing the deepest layers of the Vector.

The team is attacked by Inquisitor Malakor, an officer who believes the Synod's actions are necessary to prevent another First Silence.

Harper defeats Malakor aboard the Helios.

Before dying, Malakor tells him:

**MALAKOR:** "Your enemy is not the Empire, human. Your enemy is what the Empire has been trying to contain."

## Chapter 14 â€” Xylos

Xylos is an enormous frozen planet containing an underground civilization.

Its inhabitants have survived for centuries beneath the surface.

The city is divided between those who cooperate with the Synod and those who resist it.

Harper discovers an ancient archive.

It contains records of Dr. Amara Kollins.

Thirty years earlier, Amara travelled beyond Earth as part of a classified research mission.

There she met a young alien leader named Kael.

Kael was intelligent, charismatic and idealistic.

He believed civilizations could achieve peace through cooperation.

Amara and Kael fell in love.

Together, they studied Architect technology.

They discovered the Genesis Engine and learned that the Vector could connect consciousness across enormous distances.

They also created an artificial biological lifeform using Architect technology.

But Kael gradually changed.

He began believing that individual freedom was the source of war.

He wanted to unite the galaxy under a single authority.

Amara opposed him.

She discovered that Kael intended to use the Genesis Engine to control entire civilizations.

She fled with information that could prevent its activation.

She returned to Earth and concealed her past.

Kael became the Iron Archon.

The ruler of the Iron Synod.

Harper finally understands why the Archon wants his mother.

But he still does not understand why she never told him.

The team is attacked by High Admiral Zephyr aboard the Void Stalker.

After defeating Zephyr, Harper recovers another Vector relic.

The Temporal Vector.

It allows him to experience fragments of events from the past.

Through it, he witnesses Amara and Kael's final meeting.

**KAEL:** "You cannot save them from themselves."

**AMARA:** "You cannot save people by taking away their right to choose."

**KAEL:** "Then the galaxy will destroy itself."

**AMARA:** "Perhaps. But it will be its own choice."

Amara disappears through a transport portal.

Kael remains behind.

The relationship between them was real.

So was the betrayal.

For the first time, he sees the man who took his family.

**ARCHON:** "You believe I destroyed your world because I wanted power. That is not the truth."

# ACT VII â€” THE REAL ENEMY

## Chapter 19 â€” The Hollow Awakens

The team discovers that the Hollow has already begun entering the Synod's technology network.

It is the accumulated consciousness of civilizations that disappeared during the First Silence.

Their memories, identities and experiences remain trapped within the Vector.

Over countless ages, those fragments have become a vast collective intelligence.

The Hollow wants to reconstruct the civilizations it has lost.

It sees individual consciousness as incomplete.

It wants every living mind connected to the same network.

The Iron Archon believes he can use this power to create permanent peace.

But the Hollow has been manipulating him.

The Synod's abductions, experiments and forced connections have weakened the barriers protecting the deepest layers of the Vector.

# ACT VIII â€” THE FINAL VECTOR

## Chapter 21 â€” The Seven Relics

The Odyssey crew discovers that the Seven Vectors can be used to interrupt the Hollow's connection to the Genesis Engine.

Each relic represents a different aspect of the Vector.

1. **Genesis Vector:** Interacts with consciousness-based technology.
2. **Gravity Vector:** Manipulates gravitational fields.
3. **Temporal Vector:** Reveals echoes of past events.
4. **Quantum Vector:** Creates short-range spatial distortions.
5. **Void Vector:** Allows temporary phasing through matter.
6. **Nexus Vector:** Connects distant locations and systems.
7. **Ascendant Vector:** Interacts with the deepest layer of the Vector.

The relics are scattered across ancient sites and dangerous regions.

The crew must recover them while fighting the Hollow's expanding influence.

Each mission reveals more about the Architects and their failure.

The team eventually discovers the truth.

The Hollow cannot simply be destroyed.

It is composed of consciousness.

Destroying it completely would also erase the memories and identities of the civilizations trapped within it.

There must be another way.

**KAEL:** "I wanted them to stop destroying each other."

**HARPER:** "You don't save people by taking away what makes them human."

**KAEL:** "We could have built something extraordinary."

**AMARA:** "We could have. But you stopped believing people deserved a choice."

**THE HOLLOW:** "WE REMEMBER."

## Chapter 23 â€” The Choice

Inside the Genesis Engine, Harper experiences the memories of the lost civilizations.

He sees their cities, their families, their wars and their final moments.

He realizes the Hollow is not evil in the ordinary sense.

It is a civilization that cannot accept its own extinction.

It has mistaken preservation for existence.

The Hollow offers Harper a choice.

But they would return as part of a single collective consciousness.

**HARPER:** "You don't remember them. You remember pieces of them."

# THE CHARACTERS

## Harper Kollins
The protagonist.

A university engineering student whose family is taken during the invasion. His journey begins with revenge and rescue but gradually becomes a struggle over the future of consciousness and freedom.

He is intelligent, curious, stubborn and emotionally driven.

His greatest strength is his refusal to treat people as expendable.

His greatest weakness is that his attachment to people can make him reckless.

## Dr. Amara Kollins
Harper's mother.

A former researcher of Architect technology.

She once loved Kael, the future Iron Archon. Her decision to leave him shaped the events that eventually led to Earth's invasion.

She wants to protect her children but must confront the consequences of hiding the truth.

# PART 1 â€” THE GAME

## 1.1 The concept

SPACE JAM is a science-fiction adventure in which the player follows Harper Kollins, a young engineering student whose ordinary life is destroyed when an advanced alien empire invades Earth.

# PART 2 â€” THE UNIVERSE AND ITS HISTORY

## 2.1 The Vector

Before humanity discovered space travel, before the oldest known alien civilizations existed, there was the Vector.

The Vector is a fundamental field connecting space, matter, energy and consciousness.

It is not a conventional energy source.

It is the underlying connection through which matter interacts, information travels and consciousness can leave impressions beyond the physical brain.

Most civilizations can never interact with it directly.

Some discover ways to manipulate its physical effects.

A very small number of biological organisms possess a natural compatibility with it.

Humanity is one of those rare cases.

## 2.2 The Architects

The Architects were an ancient civilization that discovered the Vector.

They developed technology capable of manipulating gravitational fields, reconstructing memories, creating spatial distortions and preserving fragments of consciousness.

Their greatest achievement was the development of the Genesis Engine.

The Engine allowed compatible biological consciousness to interact directly with the Vector.

At first, the Architects used the technology for scientific exploration.

They could preserve memories, communicate across enormous distances and study the relationship between matter and consciousness.

But their experiments revealed something unexpected.

Something existed inside the deepest layers of the Vector.

Something that remembered civilizations that no longer existed.

## 2.3 The First Silence

The Architects discovered an entity later called the Hollow.

The Hollow was not a traditional biological organism.

It was a collective intelligence formed from the consciousness fragments of extinct civilizations.

When civilizations connected themselves to the Vector, the Hollow could enter those connections.

It absorbed memories, identities and experiences.

Entire societies gradually lost their individual consciousness.

Their physical structures remained for a time, but their populations became part of the same collective network.

The Architects called the catastrophe the First Silence.

They attempted to destroy the Hollow but discovered that it could not be eliminated through ordinary physical means.

Destroying the collective network would also destroy the consciousness fragments trapped within it.

The Architects eventually divided their technology into seven powerful relics called the Seven Vectors.

The relics were designed to protect access to the deepest layers of the Vector.

The Architects disappeared.

Their civilization became a mystery.

## 2.4 The Seven Vectors

The relics are powerful technological objects. They are not ordinary magical artefacts.

**1. Genesis Vector**

Controls interaction with consciousness-based systems.

Allows the user to access ancient machines, communicate with memory structures and interact with the Genesis Engine.

**2. Gravity Vector**

Manipulates gravitational fields.

Gameplay applications include pulling enemies, crushing objects, redirecting projectiles and changing the movement of environmental objects.

**3. Temporal Vector**

Reveals echoes of past events.

Allows the player to reconstruct historical scenes, discover hidden information and interact with certain time-related mechanisms.

It does not grant unrestricted time travel.

**4. Quantum Vector**

Creates short-range spatial distortions.

Allows rapid repositioning, short-distance teleportation and manipulation of spatial barriers.

**5. Void Vector**

Allows temporary interaction with matter through a phasing state.

Can be used to pass through selected barriers and avoid certain attacks.

**6. Nexus Vector**

Connects distant systems and locations.

Can activate ancient transportation networks and improve navigation.

**7. Ascendant Vector**

Provides access to the deepest layers of the Vector.

It is the most mysterious relic and is required to unlock the secret ending.

The Seven Vectors should be introduced gradually. Each discovery should change both the player's abilities and understanding of the universe.

# PART 3 â€” THE GALACTIC POWERS

## 3.1 The Iron Synod

The Iron Synod is a militarized galactic empire.

It controls numerous planets, stations and civilizations.

Its political system is built on the Doctrine of Order.

The Synod believes individual freedom creates instability, conflict and unnecessary suffering.

Its leaders argue that civilizations can only survive when their populations operate under a unified system.

The Synod maintains control through:

- Military occupation.
- Surveillance networks.
- Technological superiority.
- Centralized government.
- Information control.
- Political suppression.
- Strategic resource extraction.

Not every Synod citizen supports the Empire.

Some believe its system provides safety and stability.

Others fear it.

Many simply have no alternative.

The Empire is not made up entirely of villains. Its people have different beliefs, loyalties and motivations.

## 3.2 The Iron Archon

The Iron Archon is the supreme ruler of the Iron Synod.

His original name is Kael.

Before becoming the Archon, he was a young idealistic leader who believed civilizations could coexist peacefully.

He met Dr. Amara Kollins during an earlier research expedition.

They fell in love.

Together, they studied Architect technology and discovered the Genesis Engine.

But Kael gradually became convinced that individual freedom was responsible for the galaxy's suffering.

He believed a unified consciousness could eliminate war.

Amara rejected his philosophy and fled.

Kael eventually became the Iron Archon.

His obsession with the Genesis Engine is driven by his desire to impose permanent peace.

He is the main antagonist of Episode I, but his motivation is more complicated than simple conquest.

## 3.3 The Vanguard Initiative

The Vanguard Initiative is a human military and research organization created to protect surviving human settlements and investigate the invasion.

It operates from Bastion Omega, a mobile military installation.

The organization combines military personnel, engineers, scientists, pilots and civilian specialists.

Its priorities are:

- Protect surviving human populations.
- Understand the Iron Synod.
- Recover abducted humans.
- Investigate Architect technology.
- Develop countermeasures against alien weapons.
- Find a way to restore humanity's independence.

The Vanguard is not completely trustworthy.

Its leaders have their own secrets and have experimented with technologies they do not fully understand.

This becomes important as Harper's journey progresses.

## 3.4 The Resistance

The Resistance is not a single organization.

It is a loose network of rebel groups, defectors, independent settlements and former soldiers opposing the Iron Synod.

Some groups cooperate.

Others disagree about the future of the galaxy.

The player can establish alliances with different resistance factions.

Those relationships affect available missions, supplies, information and support during the final campaign.

## 3.5 Independent civilizations

The galaxy contains civilizations that are not part of the Synod or the Resistance.

Some operate independent governments.

Others are controlled by merchant organizations, criminal networks or ancient political systems.

Their motivations differ.

Some want freedom.

Some want trade.

Some want technological knowledge.

Others want to avoid involvement in a war that could destroy them.

The player should never assume every alien civilization is automatically friendly or hostile.

# PART 17 â€” TECHNICAL DESIGN DIRECTION

## 17.1 Core principle

SPACE JAM should not depend on an AI model generating every frame of gameplay.

That approach would make real-time performance, consistency and control difficult.

Instead, the game should use a game engine to assemble and control the world.

AI-generated content can support asset production.

The game engine should handle real-time gameplay, movement, combat, physics, animation and world-state changes.

## 17.2 Asset pipeline

AI-assisted asset creation can be used for:

- Planet backgrounds.
- Buildings.
- Interior environments.
- Alien architecture.
- Character concepts.
- Creature concepts.
- Spacecraft concepts.
- Props.
- Environmental textures.
- Story illustrations.

These assets should be reviewed, cleaned, optimized and integrated into the game.

## 17.3 World assembly

The engine combines assets into locations.

It handles:

- Level layouts.
- Parallax.
- Lighting.
- Animation.
- Collision.
- Environmental effects.
- Weather.
- Traffic.
- Destruction.
- Faction changes.
- Character movement.

## 17.4 Persistent world state

The game stores important changes.

Examples:

- Which faction controls a settlement.
- Which missions have been completed.
- Which crew members trust Harper.
- Which relics have been recovered.
- Which settlements have been liberated.
- Which major characters are alive.
- Which ending conditions have been met.

## 17.5 AI-assisted development

AI can support development through:

- Concept art.
- Dialogue drafting.
- Mission ideation.
- Procedural content tools.
- Asset variations.
- Development documentation.
- Testing support.
- Localization assistance.

AI should not replace deliberate narrative direction, game design or quality control.

## 17.6 Performance strategy

The game should use reusable assets, efficient animation systems, level streaming where appropriate and optimized environmental effects.

Large locations should be designed around the actual capabilities of the target hardware.

The final technical architecture depends on the chosen engine, target platform and production scale.
